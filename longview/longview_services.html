What We Cover

Every area where physicians lose money.

We mapped the financial picture of a physician or dentist from training to retirement — and built a practice that addresses every area where the wrong decision is expensive.

Student Loan Strategy

PSLF, IDR, SAVE, refinancing — we model every scenario and build a repayment strategy that survives rule changes. For physicians carrying $200k–$500k in debt, this single decision is often worth more than any investment we'll ever make. Especially urgent: the PSLF/SAVE landscape changed. If your strategy is 2+ years old, it needs a review.

Tax Planning (Not Just Filing)

Filing is not planning. We work year-round to reduce your tax burden — income, practice, investments, estate — not just document it in April. Most physicians with attending-level income are over-paying taxes by $15,000–$40,000 per year from strategies they don't know to use.

Retirement & Financial Independence

Not "retire and play golf." Work-optional — so you can practice medicine on your own terms, not because you have to. We model your number, your timeline, and the exact savings rate needed to get there.

Investment Management

Evidence-based. Low-cost. Built around your plan, not around products we're incentivized to sell. Asset allocation, tax-location strategy, and coordination across every account you own.

Insurance & Risk Analysis

Disability. Malpractice. Life. Umbrella. We review what you have, flag what you're missing, and — because we earn no commissions — we'll tell you when you have too much. (Most attending physicians are over-insured on life and under-insured on disability.)

Practice Transitions & Business Planning

Partnership buy-in. PE acquisition decisions. Practice ownership. Selling. These events restructure your entire financial picture. We model every scenario before you sign anything.

Why "comprehensive" isn't a marketing word for us
