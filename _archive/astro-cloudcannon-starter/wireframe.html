2 · The Problem — agitateICP copy

Heading: "Does this sound familiar?" Then the prospect's real pains, in their voice.

"You earn more than ever and somehow feel less in control."

"Every April the tax bill blindsides you."

"You have a pile of accounts, but no actual plan."

Replaces "Who We Help." Persona framing describes the visitor. Problem framing makes them feel seen.

3 · Why It Keeps Happening — the mechanismICP copy

The enemy. Example: "Most of the advice you have been given came from someone paid to sell you a product. Commission-driven advice is not built to serve you. It is built to move inventory."

This is the DR pivot. Name why the prospect is stuck so the solution lands as the escape, not just another pitch.

Results the conversion moment

Not a unique page per person. Results is a blanket template with 3 band versions (adjustable, 2 to 4). The assessment score buckets the prospect into one band, that version loads, and it names their problem and presents the matching solution. Same DR logic as the homepage, continued onto this page.
